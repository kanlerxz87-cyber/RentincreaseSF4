Direct answer: individual owners use the Housing Inventory and license process

For a covered rent-controlled unit, an individual owner must report into the Housing Inventory and have a current Rent Increase License before an annual or banked increase takes effect. No individual-owner or small-building exemption was found on the official pages reviewed. The official condition is the effective date; the safest order is report, verify license, check the tenancy, then serve notice.

  1. Confirm coverage privately with the exact address/APN.
  2. Report each unit at the Rent Board Portal.
  3. Verify the license for the intended effective date.
  4. Check lease type, anniversary, history, base rent and retaliation risk.
  5. Calculate only supported amounts; serve and document a compliant notice.
