Start here: do I need a Rent Board license as an individual owner?

Yes. A landlord must have a current Rent Increase License on file with the SF Rent Board before an annual or banked rent increase can take effect, and every owner of residential property must report into the Rent Board Housing Inventory first. Owners of buildings with fewer than 10 residential units have had to report since March 1, 2023. No individual-owner, small-portfolio or fewer-than-10-unit exemption was found in the official sources.

Reporting is free and is done in the Rent Board Portal. A notice that goes into effect while the unit is unlicensed may be null and void, and the tenant may be owed a refund. S01 · S07 · S08 S53

Second, separate obligation: the Rent Board Fee is not the license. Every owner pays it per unit ($59.00 for 2026-2027, due March 1) whether or not the unit is rent-controlled, an exemption must be requested every year (2027 deadline December 14, 2026), and up to 50% may be recovered from tenants - all on the Rent Board Fee page. How to read this dashboard
