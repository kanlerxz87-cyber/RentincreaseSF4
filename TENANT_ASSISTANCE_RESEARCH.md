# Tenant review and assistance: independent official-source research

Checked 2026-11-07 UTC. Informational only; this is not legal advice or an attorney's review. This note contains no tenant identifiers. It does not establish any unit's coverage, license status, increase history, or eligibility.

| Topic | Verified finding / practical implication | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tenant counseling for rent notices | The City directs tenants with rent-increase notices to tenant counseling organizations, including the Housing Rights Committee at 415-703-8644. Counseling is not necessarily attorney representation; confirm the service directly. | [Mayor's Office: Tenant and Landlord Resources](https://www.sf.gov/tenant-and-landlord-resources) |
| Free attorney assistance and its scope | The City directs tenants who have received eviction court papers to Eviction Defense Collaborative immediately for free legal help, 415-659-9184 or legal@evictiondefense.org. This source does not promise free attorney representation for every ordinary rent-increase dispute. | [Mayor's Office: Tenant and Landlord Resources](https://www.sf.gov/tenant-and-landlord-resources); [Rent Board: Evictions](https://www.sf.gov/evictions-san-francisco) |
| Rent Board counselor | The Rent Board offers counseling on the ordinance and mediations; it says it cannot provide legal advice or arbitrate discrimination or retaliation cases. Contact 415-252-4600. A counselor's information is not attorney approval. | [About the Rent Board](https://www.sf.gov/departments--rent-board--about) |
| Tenant challenge | For a unit covered by SF rent control, a tenant may file a free Tenant Petition and Form C to challenge an unlawful increase or request a lawful-rent determination; the City states no filing time limit for this petition. Documents showing rent history and notices are relevant. | [Rent Board: Report an unlawful rent increase](https://www.sf.gov/report-unlawful-rent-increase-or-request-review-your-rent-history) |
| Pending unlicensed increase | The City says a tenant can check the license in the portal; for an increase not yet effective, it directs the tenant to a Tenant Summary Petition. If an annual or banked increase takes effect without a current license, it may be null and void and a refund may be due. | [Rent Board: Notice of Owner Noncompliance](https://www.sf.gov/notice-of-owner-noncompliance-with-housing-inventory) |
| Hearing and representation | In the Form C process, the Rent Board says parties may have an attorney or other authorized representative; it sends a completed petition to the landlord and other named parties, and an administrative law judge issues a written decision. A petition does not require the owner's consent. | [Rent Board: Report an unlawful rent increase](https://www.sf.gov/report-unlawful-rent-increase-or-request-review-your-rent-history) |
| Separate owner legal advice | A Rent Board counselor is not an attorney for the owner. Seek independent qualified counsel for case-specific notice, documentation, fair-housing and retaliation issues; tenant counsel represents the tenant, not the owner. This is a precaution, not a claim that counsel has approved an increase. | [About the Rent Board](https://www.sf.gov/departments--rent-board--about); [Mayor's Office: Tenant and Landlord Resources](https://www.sf.gov/tenant-and-landlord-resources) |

## Review flags and suggested integration

- **Not verified:** that a particular tenant qualifies for free counsel for a rent-increase-only matter, or that an attorney has reviewed or approved any proposal. Do not imply universal free representation or promise an outcome. [City resource](https://www.sf.gov/tenant-and-landlord-resources)
- **Not verified:** current licenses, original tenancy date, rent and service history, prior notices, repairs, facts necessary to establish coverage, or the effect of a roommate change for either unit. These require nonpublic records and case-specific analysis; do not publish personal records. [Rent Board inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory); [Rent Board petition](https://www.sf.gov/report-unlawful-rent-increase-or-request-review-your-rent-history)
- To help withstand scrutiny, preserve contemporaneous neutral, unit-specific records of lawful base rent, eligibility date, license on effective date, rate, calculations and service; apply neutral criteria without retaliation or discrimination. This is a risk-reduction suggestion, not a guarantee of compliance or freedom from challenge. [Rent Board petition](https://www.sf.gov/report-unlawful-rent-increase-or-request-review-your-rent-history); [About the Rent Board](https://www.sf.gov/departments--rent-board--about)
