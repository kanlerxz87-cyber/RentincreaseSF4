## 2. Subtle Legal Nuances Verified in T44-A

1. **Civil Code § 1942.5(g) good-faith ground statement in notice when inside the 180-day window:**
   - Verified directly from `CIV 1942.5` chunk 1 (`S57`): Subdivision `(g)` states that if a lessor increases rent within the periods prescribed in subdivision `(a)` (the 180-day window) or subdivision `(d)`, the notice of rent increase (and any pleading/statement of issues in arbitration) must state the ground upon which the lessor, in good faith, seeks to increase rent, and if controverted, the lessor bears the burden of establishing its truth at trial or hearing.
2. **Master tenant vs. Co-tenant vs. Subtenant distinctions on Unit 1 (`S16`, `S17`, `S37`):**
   - Verified directly from `S16` and `S17`:
     - Under **Costa-Hawkins (`CIV 1954.53(d)(2)-(3)`, `S37`)** and **SF Rent Board guidance (`S16`)**, so long as `Tenant A` (an original occupant from the February 1, 2025 lease) permanently resides in Unit 1, **no** vacancy-decontrol or Section 6.14 market-rate reset is permitted (`CIV 1954.53(d)(3)`).
     - Even if `Tenant A` later vacates, if `Tenant D` (added on the April 1, 2026 "continuing tenancy" at $3,995/month) signed a written or oral agreement with the landlord or became a **co-tenant** by the landlord's acceptance of rent (`S16`), `Tenant D` is **not** subject to a Costa-Hawkins unlimited rent increase regardless of when `Tenant D` moved in; and a **Section 6.14** increase would require that the landlord timely served a written 6.14 notice on `Tenant D` within a reasonable time (generally 60 days) after knowing of `Tenant D`'s occupancy (`S16`).
     - And under `S01`, `S17`, and `S18`, charging any extra rent merely because a roommate was added or replaced is strictly unlawful and void.
