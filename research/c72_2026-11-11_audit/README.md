## 1. Forefront Executive Summary: Must an Individual Property Owner Inform the SF Rent Board & Obtain a Rent Increase License Before Raising Rent?

**Direct Answer: YES — with no exemption for individual ("mom-and-pop") owners or 2-unit buildings.**

Under **San Francisco Administrative Code § 37.15** ([SF.gov Sec. 37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing)), **Rent Board Rules & Regulations §§ 13.10–13.14** ([SF.gov Part XIII Rules](https://www.sf.gov/reports--december-2022--rent-board-rules-and-regulations-part-xiii-reporting-obligations-under-rent)), and the **Rent Board Housing Inventory** ([SF.gov Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory)), **every** owner of a rent-controlled residential unit in San Francisco — including an individual non-corporate owner of a 2-unit 1940 building (covered since **March 1, 2023**) — must report each unit to the Rent Board's Housing Inventory and hold an active **Rent Increase License** before any annual allowable (`§ 37.3(a)(1)`) or banked (`§ 37.3(a)(2)`) rent increase takes effect.
