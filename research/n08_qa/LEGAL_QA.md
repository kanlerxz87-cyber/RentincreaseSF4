## Direct answer to keep at the front

For the described rent-controlled units, an individual owner is not exempt from the Housing Inventory/Rent Increase License process. Report each unit through the official Rent Board Portal, confirm the generated license, and make sure it is current on the increase's effective date. Reporting is free; the separate annual Rent Board Fee is not the license. For an ordinary annual/banked increase, no separate Rent Board petition is described on the official pages. Special petition-based increases and §6.14/Costa-Hawkins situations have different rules. Sources: [Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory), [rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco), [§37.15](https://www.sf.gov/information--sec-3715-reporting-obligations-licensing), [Rent Board Fee](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-fee).

The current ordinary SF rate verified on the official page is **1.6% for March 1, 2026–February 28, 2027**. On the supplied rents, that is **$63.92 → $4,058.92** for Unit 1 and **$50.40 → $3,200.40** for Unit 2, only if the supplied rent is the current base rent and all timing/license/coverage conditions are met. These are calculations, not notices or legal approval. The next rate, actual current rent history, license status and Unit 1 occupant status remain unverified. Sources: [SF rate and calculation rule](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco).
