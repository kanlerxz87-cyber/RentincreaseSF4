## 5. Plain-language skeleton — do not serve with blanks

```text
NOTICE OF RENT INCREASE (DRAFT — NOT FOR SERVICE)

To: [verified tenant placeholders / private names kept out of this public repo]
Unit: [private unit identifier kept out of this public repo]
Notice date: [date]

Current verified base rent: $[amount] per month.
Effective [date], the base rent will change to $[amount] per month.
Dollar increase: $[amount]. Percentage increase: [percent]%.

Breakdown:
- Annual allowable portion: [percent]% / $[amount], under SF Admin Code §37.3(a)(1).
- Banked portion: [percent]% / $[amount], based on [dates], if any.
- Other authorized components: [identify each separately, with required forms/calculations], if any.

This draft does not include any unverified passthrough, petition-based increase,
occupant surcharge, or market-rate reset.

Owner/agent: [private contact kept out of this public repo]
```

The skeleton is a drafting aid, not an official Rent Board form. The reviewed Forms Center sections did not identify a mandatory annual rent-increase notice template; Form 583 is described for documents delivered to other parties **in a case**, not as an annual-notice filing requirement. See [Forms Center](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-forms-forms-center) and B87-F05.
