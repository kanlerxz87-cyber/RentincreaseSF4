## 3. Date arithmetic guard

Do not fill an effective date from the lease start alone. First verify the anniversary, the combined percentage, the service method and any longer contract/statutory period. For a verified personal-delivery date, the conservative calendar check is at least 30 days (or 90 days if the combined increase exceeds 10%). For California mail, add 5 calendar days. Then confirm the date is on/after the verified anniversary and within the correct rate window.
