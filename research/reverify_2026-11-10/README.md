## Exact text captured (verbatim fragments)

- **Licence, individual owners:** "A landlord must get a rent increase license before imposing annual allowable and/or banked rent increases on a tenant." / "To get a rent increase license, a landlord must first report into the Rent Board Housing Inventory." / "This is effective July 1, 2022 (or March 1, 2023 for condominiums and buildings with 1-9 residential units)." (S01)
- **Housing Inventory duty:** "All owners of residential property are required to report." / "The Rent Board does not assess monetary penalties for filing after the deadline." / "your rent increase notice will not be enforceable without having a rent increase license on the effective date of the notice" / "The information entered in the housing inventory submission is public." (S07)
- **Ordinance:** "shall have a license to impose annually a rent increase which does not exceed a tenant's base rent by more than 60% of said published increase. In no event, however, shall the allowable annual increase be greater than 7%." (S21) / "if the landlord has not substantially complied ... the license to impose rent increases shall be temporarily suspended ... but a tenant shall not be obligated to pay the increased rent for months during the period of suspension." (S53)
- **Rate and notice:** "The allowed rent increase percentage in San Francisco is 1.6%." / "effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027" / "30-day written notice" / "90-day written notice if the increase (either by itself or combined with another increase in the same year) is more than 10%" / "If the notice is mailed, the landlord must give the tenant 5 more days." / "Rent increases cannot be rounded up to the nearest dollar." (S01)
- **Banking:** "A skipped increase is only banked if at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase." / "They cannot be compounded or prorated." (S05)
- **AB 1482:** "The maximum rent increase for units under AB 1482 from August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027 is 8.8% in San Francisco." / "This does NOT apply for units under the rent increase limitations of the San Francisco Rent Ordinance." (S27)
- **Two-unit exemption:** "A property containing two separate dwelling units within a single structure in which the owner occupied one of the units as the owner's principal place of residence at the beginning of the tenancy, so long as the owner continues in occupancy, and neither unit is an accessory dwelling unit or a junior accessory dwelling unit." (S36, 1947.12(d)(6))
- **Retaliation:** "may not ... increase the rent ... within 180 days"; (b) once in any 12-month period; (g) good-faith ground stated in the notice, to be proved if controverted; (h)(2) "$100 ... $2,000" where fraud, oppression or malice; (i) attorney's fees "if either party requests attorney's fees upon the initiation of the action". (S57)
- **Discriminatory effect:** practice "necessary to achieve one or more substantial, legitimate, nondiscriminatory interests" and those interests "could not be served by another practice that has a less discriminatory effect". (S42)
- **Fee:** "For the 2026-2027 tax year, the fee is: $59.00 per dwelling unit; $29.50 per guest unit" / "March 1 of each year" / "maximum penalty of 15%" / "The deadline to submit a Request for Exemption for the 2027 Rent Board fee is December 14, 2026." (S56)
- **Pending ordinance:** "The San Francisco Board of Supervisors is considering legislation (File No. 261020) that would temporarily limit the combined amount of banked rent increases and capital improvement rent increases that may be imposed on rent-controlled units to 10% per year." / "would apply to banked rent increases noticed on or after September 15, 2026, and capital improvement rent increases based on petitions filed with the Rent Board on or after September 15, 2026." / "This legislation has not been enacted and is subject to change." (S01/S02/S05, posted November 9, 2026). Legislative record: "Ordinance amending the Administrative Code to provide that banked rent increases and capital improvement rent increases for rent-controlled units may not increase a tenant's rent by more than a total of 10% per year, for the next three years"; Introduced 9/15/2026; Status "Pending Committee Action"; no Enactment date. (S71)
