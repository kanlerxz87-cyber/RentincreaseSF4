## Conditions before any figure above could be used

1. Verify the unit is covered by SF rent control using the exact address (not supplied). [SF.gov rental laws](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-san-francisco-rental-laws) / [PIM](https://sfplanninggis.org/PIM/).
2. Verify a current Rent Increase License on the effective date. [Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory) / [Owner Noncompliance](https://www.sf.gov/notice-of-owner-noncompliance-with-housing-inventory).
3. Verify at least 12 months since the last increase and the correct anniversary date. [Learn about rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco).
4. Use the rate in effect on the increase date, written notice, 30/90-day timing, +5 days if mailed, and required contents. [Learn about rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco) / [Civil Code §827](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=827&lawCode=CIV).
5. Apply a neutral, identical written policy to every eligible unit; document eligibility reasons if only one unit is increased. [24 CFR 100.500](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/subtitle-B/chapter-I/part-100/subpart-G/section-100.500) / [FEHA §12955](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=12955.&lawCode=GOV).
6. Do not use a roommate change, a long tenure below market, or an assumed vacancy-decontrol event as a reason without official eligibility and legal review. [Charging for Additional Occupants Prohibited](https://www.sf.gov/information--charging-additional-occupants-prohibited), [Special Circumstances Petitions](https://www.sf.gov/information--special-circumstances-petitions-based-rents-comparable-units), [§6.14/Costa-Hawkins](https://www.sf.gov/information--rent-increases-under-section-614-and-costa-hawkins).
