The license is an effective-date precondition

Two distinct registrations with the Rent Board

License timing and retaliation risk
AN-27		A license that exists today does not validate a notice whose effective date preceded it. California Civil Code section 1942.5 separately limits rent increases after qualifying tenantability events, subject to its conditions and exceptions.verifiedS53 S08 S57Check the effective date and the statutory conditions; no ranking of defects is established.
