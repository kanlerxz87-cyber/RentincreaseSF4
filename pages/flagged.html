F33 — Rent Board Fee and Rent Increase License are easy to conflate

They are separate obligations. The licence comes from Housing Inventory reporting; the fee is a per-unit annual payment (2026-2027: $59.00 per dwelling unit, due March 1). SF.gov states expressly that requesting a fee exemption does not satisfy Housing Inventory reporting, so an owner can be fee-paid and still unlicensed, or licensed and still owe the fee.

F47 — The proposed-cap notice, the Legistar record and the draft ordinance do not fully align on retroactivity or on how the 10% is measured

Verified 2026-11-10 from two official pages and re-verified 2026-11-11 against the Legistar attachment Leg Ver1. (1) Retroactivity: the November 9, 2026 notice says the proposal would apply to banked increases noticed on or after September 15, 2026, a date earlier than the notice and earlier than any possible enactment; the ordinance was introduced 9/15/2026. Neither page says whether an increase noticed between 9/15/2026 and enactment would be caught. (2) Duration: the notice calls the measure temporary; the Legistar title says three years; the draft text makes the limit effective through December 31, 2029 and lets landlords accumulate the excess and impose it on or after January 1, 2030. (3) Measurement: the draft adds proposed Sec. 37.3(h), which limits combined banked and capital-improvement increases to 10% of the lowest base rent in effect at any time in the 12 months before the increase takes effect, and does not state how it interacts with the separate annual allowable increase under Sec. 37.3(a)(1). The draft is NOT enacted: the Legistar status remains Pending Committee Action.
