Suggestions - review before implementing

A dedicated review queue of proposed improvements to this site and to the rent-increase decision process. Each suggestion states what it changes, why it helps, the official basis, the effort, and its review status so a reviewer can choose the best path before anything is implemented.

Informational compliance research, not legal advice. No attorney has approved a proposed increase and nothing here guarantees that a complaint, inquiry or lawsuit will not occur. Any increase is framed only as a conservative compliance checklist capable of tenant-lawyer review.

C. Suggestions on how to approach the increase itself (decision paths, not legal advice)

IDSuggestionWhy it helps the decisionOfficial source(s)EffortReview status
SUG-C1Adopt one written, neutral rule - 'on each unit's anniversary date, impose the published annual allowable increase if the licence is current' - and apply it identically to both unitsAn identical rule applied by anniversary date is the cleanest answer to the one-vs-both discrimination question, because the selection criterion is the calendar, not the tenant.
Analysis recommendation. It is not a guarantee against a complaint or a petition.		S42 S38 S01One page of policy textopen for review
SUG-C2Document the neutral reason for any unit that is NOT increased at the same time (for example: anniversary not reached, or licence not current)If the two units are treated differently, the file should show a reason that does not depend on who the tenant is. This is the practical defence in a disparate-treatment inquiry.
Contingent recommendation; depends on SUG-A2 and SUG-A5.		S42 S38 S2210 minutes per decisionopen for review
SUG-C3Do not raise rent merely because a roommate was added to Unit 1SF.gov states additional-occupant charges are an unlawful rent increase even where the lease provides for one. This is the sharpest available trap in this case.
Directly answers Q-14 and AN-07.		S01 S18 S17No action needed - avoidanceopen for review
SUG-C4Set the effective date no earlier than the later of (a) the anniversary date and (b) the date the licence is confirmed on fileRule 13.14 tests the effective date, not the service date. Sequencing the dates this way removes the nullity risk without needing to guess.
