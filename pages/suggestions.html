Adopt one written, neutral annual-increase policy for each eligible unit
SGN-01		Use an identical written standard (eligibility, anniversary date, rate, calculation) applied to each eligible unit rather than ad hoc decisions.proposedS01 S42 S38No official source requires raising both units, but identical neutral criteria reduce disparate-treatment risk. Proposed review decision: approve a single neutral policy, or document why one unit differs, before serving any notice.
Make the Notice Worksheet a standing pre-notice gate
SGN-02		Require every notice to clear the worksheet gates (coverage, license on the effective date, anniversary, rate, banking, petitions, retaliation) and record service.proposedS01 S07 S35 S06Each gate is checkable against the linked official page. Proposed review decision: adopt the worksheet as the standard pre-notice checklist for both units.
Add an annual rate-in-force check every March 1
SGN-03		Before serving a notice, confirm the allowable percentage in effect on the increase date and record the rate page date used.proposedS01 S02 S04The notice must use the rate in effect on the increase date, not the notice date, and the schedule changes each March 1. Proposed review decision: calendar a March 1 rate check and file the dated page copy.
Add a decision log recording the chosen path, sources and dates

Project-record source: it is not a government legal source. Proposed review decision: adopt a one-page decision log for both units.

Show any fee recovery as a separate fee line, never inside a rent increase
SGN-06		If recovering the 50% Rent Board Fee ($29.50 per dwelling unit for 2026-2027), bill it as a fee recovery with its own line item and wording.proposedS56 S62 S63The recovery is a fee, not rent, and penalties may not be recovered. Proposed review decision: if recovering, present it separately and keep the deposit-interest or billing method compliant.
