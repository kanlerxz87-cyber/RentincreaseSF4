Suggestions - review before implementing

A dedicated review queue of proposed improvements to this site and to the rent-increase decision process. Each suggestion states what it changes, why it helps, the official basis, the effort, and its review status so a reviewer can choose the best path before anything is implemented.

Informational compliance research, not legal advice. No attorney has approved a proposed increase and nothing here guarantees that a complaint, inquiry or lawsuit will not occur. Any increase is framed only as a conservative compliance checklist capable of tenant-lawyer review.

D. Compliance gates - make the required order of operations impossible to miss

Each proposal below adds a gate that must be answered before a notice is dated. Every one is grounded in a rule published by the San Francisco Rent Board, the Rent Ordinance, California statute or federal regulation. Contributed by the 2026-11-13 final review.
