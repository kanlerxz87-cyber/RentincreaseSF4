SF Rent Board — The Rent Board Fee
S56
Separate from the Rent Increase License: every residential dwelling unit owner must pay a per-unit Rent Board Fee unless exempt. 2026-2027: $59.00 per dwelling unit, $29.50 per guest unit. Due March 1 each year; 5% monthly late penalty to a 15% maximum; unpaid balances referred to the Bureau of Delinquent Revenue after June 1. If the fee is paid in full, 50% ($29.50 per dwelling unit) may be recovered from tenants in occupancy as of November 1 of the tax year; penalties may not be recovered. 'Requesting a Rent Board Fee exemption does not satisfy Rent Board Housing Inventory reporting requirements.' Re-verified 2026-11-07.rule checked

The exemption needs both a post-June-13-1979 first Certificate of Occupancy and no residential use before it. Re-verified 2026-11-07.rule checked

SF Rent Board — Partial Exemption for Certain Single-Family Homes and Condominiums Under Costa-Hawkins
S60
Single-family homes and condominiums are exempt from the rent increase limitations only if the tenancy commenced on or after January 1, 1996. A dwelling with a legal or illegal in-law unit constitutes a two-unit building and is NOT exempt unless both units are rented together as a single tenancy. A single-family dwelling is not exempt if another residential structure is on the same lot. Exempted units still owe the annual Rent Board fee. Re-verified 2026-11-07.rule checked
