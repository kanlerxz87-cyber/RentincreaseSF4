SF Rent Board | Recover part of your Rent Board fee from the tenant
S62		A landlord who has paid the Rent Board fee in full may collect 50% (excluding late penalties) from the tenant(s) residing in the unit, and only if the tenant resided there on or before November 1 of the year before the fee became due. Recovery is by deducting the amount from the annual security-deposit interest payment, or by billing directly only where there is no or insufficient deposit held or the landlord already pays interest annually. The bill must state the fee amount for each year, any deposit interest owed, that the purpose is to fund the Rent Board, and that the fee is due within 30 days. Unrecovered fees may be banked. A tenant's failure to pay is not a just cause for eviction; collection is through Small Claims Court. Re-verified 2026-11-08.verifiedS62Local
