SF Rent Board | Evictions in San Francisco
S61		Most residential tenants have just-cause eviction protection, including in buildings built after 1979. A landlord must follow the legal process (written notice, unlawful detainer lawsuit, court judgment, Sheriff removal) and may not lock out, remove possessions, shut off utilities or harass. The Rent Ordinance generally allows tenants to replace departing roommates or add occupants even where a written lease prohibits it. A tenant who receives an Unlawful Detainer should contact the Eviction Defense Collaborative at 415-659-9184 for free legal help; the Rent Board (415-252-4600) gives general information and a referral listing. SF Rent Board | Recover part of your Rent Board fee from the tenant
S62		A landlord who has paid the Rent Board fee in full may collect 50% (excluding late penalties) from the tenant(s) residing in the unit, and only if the tenant resided there on or before November 1 of the year before the fee became due. Recovery is by deducting the amount from the annual security-deposit interest payment, or by billing directly only where there is no or insufficient deposit held or the landlord already pays interest annually. The bill must state the fee amount for each year, any deposit interest owed, that the purpose is to fund the Rent Board, and that the fee is due within 30 days. Unrecovered fees may be banked. A tenant's failure to pay is not a just cause for eviction; collection is through Small Claims Court. Re-verified 2026-11-08.verifiedS62Local

California Civil Code | CIV 1947.7 (substantial compliance with a local rent-registration system)
S91		Direct fetch_page 2026-11-13. The Legislature's stated intent is 'to limit the imposition of penalties and sanctions against an owner of residential rental units where that person has attempted in good faith to fully comply with the regulatory processes' of a local rent program. Subdivision (b): an owner 'in substantial compliance with an ordinance or charter that controls or establishes a system of controls on the price at which residential rental units may be offered for rent or lease and which requires the registration of rents, or any regulation adopted pursuant thereto, shall not be assessed a penalty or any other sanction for noncompliance', with restitution to the tenant or recovery of registration/filing fees due to the local agency as the exclusive remedies; 'substantial compliance' means a good-faith attempt plus timely cure after notice of a deficiency. Subdivision (c): 'all annual rent adjustments which may have been denied during the period of the owner's noncompliance shall be restored prospectively once the owner is in compliance'.
