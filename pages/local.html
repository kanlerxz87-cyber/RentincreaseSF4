Ordinance text 37.15
LOC-14		Owners report under penalty of perjury; condominiums and units in buildings under 10 units report from March 1, 2023 and annually by March 1; compliant owners receive a license to impose rent increases; otherwise the license is suspended and the tenant is not obligated to pay the increased rent for months during the suspensionverifiedS53 S20Added by Ord. 265-20; not a rental registry under Civil Code 1947.7-1947.8.
Rent Board Fee is separate from the license
LOC-20		Every residential dwelling unit owner pays a per-unit fee unless exempt: 2026-2027 is $59.00 per dwelling unit, due March 1, 5% late penalty per month to a 15% maximum; a fee exemption does not satisfy Housing Inventory reportingverifiedS56Two separate obligations; see flag F33.
Recovering half the fee
LOC-21		If the fee is paid in full, 50% ($29.50 per dwelling unit for 2026-2027) may be recovered from tenants in occupancy as of November 1 of the tax year; penalties may not be recoveredverifiedS56Not a rent increase; identify it separately on any statement.
Units built after June 13, 1979
LOC-22		Exempt from the rent increase limitations only, if the first Certificate of Occupancy issued after June 13, 1979 and there was no prior residential use; just-cause eviction protection and the Rent Board fee still applyverifiedS59The owner-supplied 1940 year would fall before the cutoff if confirmed.
In-law unit or second structure on the lot
LOC-23		A dwelling with a legal or illegal in-law unit is a two-unit building and is not exempt unless both units are rented together as a single tenancy; a single-family dwelling is not exempt if another residential structure is on the same lotverifiedS60Two rented units is consistent with coverage; see flag F35.
30-day update duty
LOC-24		A change in the owner or designated manager name or business contact information must be reported within 30 daysverifiedS53Separate from the annual March 1 update.
License scope
LOC-15		License rules apply only to rent-controlled units; SF.gov says the license is not required for 6.14/Costa-Hawkins increases or petition-based passthroughsverifiedS08 S16 S09Those increases have separate rules and remedies.
Housing Inventory data is public
LOC-16		Submission information is public, including name and business contact detailsverifiedS07 S53Privacy consideration before filing.
Multiple-year increases
LOC-17		Landlords are not required to file a petition to impose multi-year banked increases; add the allowable percentages; no compounding; no rounding upverifiedS5430-day notice (35 if mailed).
Where to file and check
LOC-18		Rent Board Portal account; report inventory; look up license status by address or APN; paper filings by email or office drop-offverifiedS55 S08Office open 9-4 weekdays; counseling 415-252-4600.
