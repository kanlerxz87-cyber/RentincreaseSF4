Who pays, how much, and when

Who pays the Rent Board Fee
FEE-01		The owner of every residential dwelling unit or guest unit pays a Rent Board Fee each year unless a current Rent Board exemption or an Assessor-Recorder Homeowners' Exemption is on fileverifiedS56 S58This fee is a separate obligation from the Rent Increase License; paying one does not satisfy the other. Sec. 37A.1 and Sec. 37A.4 of the Administrative Code.
Which units are charged
FEE-02		All residential units unless currently exempt, including single-family homes and condominiums, units built after 1979, vacant units, units occupied by friends or family, and units exempt from the rent-increase limits under Costa-HawkinsverifiedS56 S58A tenant-occupied unit is not covered by the owner-occupied exemption, which Sec. 37A.1(f) limits to an owner-occupied unit not rented at any time with an affidavit on file.
Amount for the 2026-2027 tax year
FEE-03		$59.00 per dwelling unit; $29.50 per guest unit (SRO / residential hotel room)verifiedS56 S59The 2026-27 figure is the one published on the SF.gov fee page; Form 573's newest row is tax year 7/25-6/26 and also shows $59.00 (flag F34).
Deadline and late penalty
FEE-04		Due March 1 each year; a 5% penalty is added each month after March 1, capped at 15%verifiedS56 S58Same March 1 date as the Housing Inventory report, but a separate filing.
If the fee is still unpaid after June 1
FEE-05		Unpaid fees are referred to the Bureau of Delinquent Revenue with additional costs added to the principal chargeverifiedS56 S58The same SF.gov page's timeline describes a July Notice of Delinquent Payment and 30 days to pay first - wording differs (flag F31).
How to pay
FEE-06		Online by credit card or eCheck through the Rent Board Portal (preferred), in person at the Treasurer and Tax Collector, City Hall Room 140, or by mail to San Francisco Rent Board, PO Box 7429, San Francisco, CA 94120-7429; a dishonored payment adds a $50.00 Payment Failure FeeverifiedS56No payments are accepted at the Rent Board office.
Exemption request window for the 2027 fee
FEE-07		Request for Exemption due December 14, 2026; units without a valid and approved exemption are billed for the fee, due March 1, 2027verifiedS56 S60The January 2027 Annual Notice is the invoice; the October 2026 notice is informational only.
An exemption request is annual and is not inventory reporting
FEE-08		An exemption request must be submitted every year; requesting a fee exemption does not satisfy Housing Inventory reporting and reporting into the Housing Inventory does not satisfy a fee-exemption requestverifiedS56 S60Two separate filings, two separate consequences.
Owner-occupied exemption is narrow
FEE-09		Available only for an owner-occupied unit that is not rented at any time, supported by a Homeowners' Exemption or an affidavit filed with the Rent Board; vacant units and units occupied only by relatives not on title do not qualifyverifiedS56 S58Not available for either rented unit in this case.

Recovering 50% from the tenant

ItemValue / findingStatusOfficial source(s)Notes / flag
Maximum recoverable from the tenant
FEE-10		Up to 50% of the annual fee, excluding penalties, and only from tenants in occupancy on November 1 of the applicable tax year, and only after the owner has paid the fee in fullverifiedS57 S58Sec. 37A.6 sets the cap; for the 2026-27 fee that is up to $29.50 per dwelling unit.
How the 50% may be collected
FEE-11		By deducting it from the next security-deposit interest payment; a direct bill is allowed only where there is no or insufficient deposit, or where interest is already paid annually, and the bill must state the amount, that the purpose is to fund the Rent Board, and that it is due within 30 daysverifiedS57 S58Sec. 37A.6(b) allows banking; the same method then applies.
Consequences if the tenant does not pay
FEE-12		Failure to pay the Rent Board fee is not a just cause for eviction; the owner must go to Small Claims Court, and the owner remains liable to the Rent Board whether or not any part is recoveredverifiedS57 S58Sec. 37A.6(d): the owner stays liable for the full fee.
Security-deposit interest rate that applies to a deduction
FEE-13		4.2% for the period March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027verifiedS02Only relevant if the fee is recovered by deducting it from deposit interest.
